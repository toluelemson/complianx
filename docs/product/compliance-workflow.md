# Compliance workflow

1. Create an organization project and register an AI system.
2. Complete the applicability and classification questionnaire.
3. Review the resulting requirements and assign owners and actions.
4. Upload evidence, retain its version and hash, and link it to requirements.
5. Request review; an authorized reviewer evaluates evidence and decisions.
6. Resolve gaps and approve the applicable requirements.
7. Generate a versioned compliance package and verify its manifest and archive integrity.

AI-generated suggestions are advisory. A human reviewer must approve compliance decisions before they are treated as finalized.
