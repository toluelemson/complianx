# Compliance workflow

Neuraldocx guides each registered AI system through five connected stages:

1. **Register** — Create the organization project, register the AI system, and
   record its responsible owner and operating context.
2. **Assess** — Complete applicability and classification questions. Results
   are preliminary until the required human review is recorded.
3. **Comply** — Map applicable requirements, assign owners and actions, upload
   versioned evidence, and resolve findings.
4. **Review** — Request review; authorized reviewers approve work or request
   changes. Rejected evidence, expiring evidence, and outstanding actions send
   work back to the appropriate earlier stage.
5. **Report** — Check readiness, generate a versioned compliance package, and
   verify its manifest and archive integrity.

The stages are iterative: a change in scope, evidence, regulation, or review
decision can require reassessment or further compliance work. A generated
document describes the reviewed state; it does not itself establish compliance.

AI-generated suggestions are advisory. A human reviewer must approve compliance decisions before they are treated as finalized.
