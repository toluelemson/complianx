# Vision

Neuraldocx is an EU AI Act compliance workspace that turns regulatory requirements into evidence-backed, reviewable, audit-ready workflows.

The product helps teams register AI systems, determine applicability, classify risk, map requirements, collect evidence, assign actions, complete human review, and export verifiable compliance packages.

Compliance conclusions remain subject to customer and professional review. Neuraldocx supports decisions; it does not provide legal advice or autonomous approval.
