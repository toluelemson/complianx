# Target customer

Neuraldocx is for organizations building, providing, deploying, or operating AI systems in the EU and for consultants supporting those organizations.

The initial customer is a small or mid-sized compliance, risk, legal, product, or security team that needs a repeatable way to classify systems and assemble evidence for review.

Consultants can use assisted setup and shared evidence workflows across multiple customer projects. Larger programs can use scoped workspaces and organization-level controls.
