# Product boundaries

Neuraldocx currently does not aim to be:

- a generic enterprise GRC platform
- a replacement for legal advice
- a fully autonomous compliance approval system
- a multi-framework suite before the EU AI Act workflow is mature
- a microservice platform or blockchain audit system

The product keeps a modular monolith, PostgreSQL-backed evidence model, organization-scoped authorization, and human approval workflow. New regulatory frameworks should reuse these foundations rather than duplicate the compliance model.
