# EU AI Act scope

EU AI Act is the first regulatory framework implemented in Neuraldocx. The workflow covers:

- AI system registration and intended purpose
- operator role and applicability questions
- prohibited and high-risk indicators
- requirement and control mapping
- evidence collection, review, and approval
- audit-ready package generation and integrity verification

Regulatory pack versions are retained with assessments and packages so conclusions can be interpreted against the content in force at that time. The architecture allows additional frameworks later without treating them as part of the initial product promise.
